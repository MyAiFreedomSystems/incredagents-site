# TakeMyCourseForMe

Extract a paid online course from **Skool** or **Circle** into ADHD-friendly
**cliff notes** (a TLDR + scannable study guide) plus a **"know enough to
build/implement it"** handoff — one skill, one entry point, both platforms.
It drives your real logged-in browser (never headless for Skool), maps the
course tree, captures every lesson (text + the downloadable ZIP/PDF files that
are the real deliverables; video and transcripts via yt-dlp in the walkthrough),
cleans transcripts, and scaffolds the cliff notes and build handoff.

**For personal study of content you've paid for. Do not redistribute the
extracted material.**

See `ONBOARDING.md` for install + a full walkthrough, `SKILL.md` for the full
playbook.
