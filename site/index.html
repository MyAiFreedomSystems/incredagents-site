Installed in two minutes. Working tonight.

$ git clone https://github.com/MyAiFreedomSystems/graphic-testimonials.git

# then tell your agent:

"Harvest my client testimonials from my YouTube channel and make branded cards."

# review what it made, swap what you don't like, publish.
